Cast a stone after the day,
as far as you can.
Rinse the salt from your skin,
extinguish the fire.
Follow the path that lies before you,
do not seek what is gone forever.
Wash the sand from your hair,
give a name to every year.
Drink the tears from your hand,
keep silent about them.
Inherit the eyes of your child,
look through them.
Cherish your secret heart,
until the end.

Travel far, drink wine, reflect,
laugh loudly, dive deep,
come back.

Dream a boat into the sun,
give it sails and wind.
Kiss a sorrowful mouth,
ever so gently,
before the day begins.
Keep a stone in your bag,
from the land where you slept,
where you sustained your wounds,
where the kingdom fell to ruin.
Retrieve the pearls from the sea,
give them away.
Fight with everything you have,
lose it all.
Then wait for the light to grow,
you have the time.

Travel far, drink wine, reflect,
laugh loudly, dive deep,
come back.
