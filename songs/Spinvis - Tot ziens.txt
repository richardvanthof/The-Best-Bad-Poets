May your children be strong and beautiful
And your tears dried by the sun
And your beauty as eternal as the sea
And your days be grand
May your book dwell in every heart
And your loves be many and full of fire
Your secret a well-kept jewel
In a black universe
May your monster never find you

A street in April
Without defining trait, without reason
Outside, all falls silent
A gentle rain from long ago

May your home await you after distant journeys
A thousand birds in your garden
And the sweet end, soft as twilight
